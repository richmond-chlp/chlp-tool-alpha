Estimate the health your climate spending buys

The Climate Health Ledger Project (CHLP) tool turns what an implementer already knows into a cost per disability-adjusted life year (DALY) averted, with a range. It should take about 30 minutes.

Nothing you type here is stored or sent. To share your entry, copy it at the foot of the page and send it to richmond@climatehealthledger.org. To carry on later, keep that copy and paste it back into the page. If you are taking part in CHLP's test, send your entry to the same address when you finish. That alone does not put it in CHLP's database: your answers on consent decide that.

First: your permission

CHLP needs your explicit permission, so the rest of the page opens once each of these three questions has an answer. You can change an answer at any time before you send your entry. Only you can give consent: a funder cannot send your entry or consent for you. Nothing is sent from this page.

Entries shared with CHLP are open to anyone. Until its database is online, CHLP sends them on request. CHLP keeps the entry as it was received, with its date. If you answer No, CHLP reads your feedback and keeps no copy of your entry.
A check compares four things with your records. They are the units delivered, the spending, the basis for the share that would have happened anyway, and the fit of the intervention and setting to the factors. Your consent covers the result whatever it shows. You see the result 30 days before it is recorded.

Possible health effects

Not sure what your program does for health? Tick each question that fits. The tool lists the health effects to look for, harms as well as benefits. If you already know, skip this step.

Possible health effects. Not an estimate. Each one is a prompt to check. A number appears only where the tool has figures or you enter your own.

What your program delivers

An intervention is what your program delivers, counted in a unit you already record. A pathway is one route from that unit to a health outcome in one group of people. An intervention can have more than one pathway.

Pick the one that fits your program. If none fits, choose "Your own intervention" and give your own figures.
For example the same fuel, a similar climate or a similar level of pollution. Name a measurement that would check it. The result is marked as extrapolated.

Tracker: what happened, quarter by quarter

Use this after a quarter has ended. Record what you delivered and spent, and the page sets it beside your estimate. Health is modeled from the units you delivered, with the factors above. The page stores nothing: copy your entry at the foot of the page to keep it, and paste it back next quarter to carry on.
