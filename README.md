# The CHLP tool (alpha)

An open tool from the Climate Health Ledger Project (CHLP). An organization that runs a climate program enters what the program delivers and what it costs, and the tool returns a cost per disability-adjusted life year (DALY) averted, with a range, and the net change in emissions beside it.

**Use it:** open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the published page. It takes about 30 minutes.

## What to know before you use it

- **Nothing is stored or sent.** The page runs in your browser. To keep your work, copy your entry at the foot of the page and paste it back later.
- **Sharing is optional.** To share an entry with CHLP, copy it and email it to richmond@climatehealthledger.org. Three questions on consent come first, and each needs an answer.
- **Every result is your own estimate.** Each result carries the label "Your own modeled estimate. CHLP has not checked it." CHLP checks a sample of shared entries against what happened.
- **Every result shows its working.** The inputs, sources and factors behind each result are on the page, so whoever reads it can check it.

## Status
